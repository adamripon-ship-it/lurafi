## Product Overview
**One-liner:** KEVIN® 3 is a Swiss presence simulator that makes an empty home look and sound lived-in from the street, so burglars pick another house.
**What it does:** A speaker-sized device placed near a window plays warm light scenes, moving human shadows and everyday household sounds from its own 32 GB storage. It runs on a weekly schedule or switches on by geofencing when you leave. It has no camera and no microphone.
**Product category:** Burglary deterrent / presence simulation (customers search: "Anwesenheitssimulation", "aanwezigheidssimulator", "simulateur de présence", "fake TV light", "make house look occupied").
**Product type:** Consumer hardware (one-time purchase) with a free companion app; optional accessory front covers.
**Business model:** Direct-to-consumer on mitipi.eu (Shopify). KEVIN 3 €579.95 one-time, lifetime software updates, 3-year warranty, free shipping in EU/CH/LI/NO/IS, 30-day returns. Front covers €29.95 each. No monitoring subscription.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** an empty-looking home is chosen by opportunists; alarms and cameras only act after the door is tested.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Lamp timers and "fake TV" boxes are recognisable patterns (same lamp, same time).
- Smart bulbs need a hub, a subscription or a working network.
- Cameras record a burglary; they do not prevent one, and they watch the family too.
- Monitored alarms cost monthly and react late.
**What it costs them:** the break-in itself, and afterwards the feeling of not being safe at home (three quarters of victims, per the KFN study cited by Introgarde).
**Emotional tension:** dread when leaving; guilt about a parent alone; not wanting to become "the paranoid one" with cameras everywhere.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** TV simulators / "FakeTV" boxes (cheap, light only, obvious pattern); Home Shadows-type shadow projectors (no sound).
**Secondary:** Philips Hue "Mimic presence" and smart-plug timers (need ecosystem; lights only); Ring/alarm kits (react after entry; Galaxus compares Kevin's price to a Ring kit with six sensors).
**Indirect:** asking a neighbour; leaving the radio on; a dog.
**How each falls short:** none combines light + moving shadow + sound in one plug-and-play unit that works without the network.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Light, moving human shadows (patented) and everyday sounds from one device.
- Plays from 32 GB on-device storage: keeps working when Wi-Fi is jammed or down.
- No camera, no microphone, nothing streamed or shared.
- Engineered and designed in Switzerland; 3-year warranty; one-time purchase, no monitoring fee.
- Weekly schedules, geofencing, your own recorded sounds; multiple properties in one app.
**Why customers choose us:** "an easy-to-use, all-in-one solution that delivers a truly spectacular show" (Galaxus, Jan 2026).

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| It can't raise an alarm. | Correct. Kevin prevents; keep your alarm for detection. Deterrence is the layer alarms lack. |
| It only covers one room. | Place it where the street looks in; a second unit covers a second facade. |
| €580 is a lot. | One-time, no monitoring fee, 3-year warranty, 30-day returns; compare with a monitored alarm over three years. |
| Setup / app friction (real reviews). | Plug in, place near the window, pair once. Three buttons on the device work without the app. Support address on every page. |
| Reviews look inflated / brand had a fake-ratings story. | Quote only verified, dated sources; state limits openly. |
| It speaks English / not French. | Say what is true: app in EN/DE/FR/NL; on-device scenes in English, German and Swiss German; you can record your own household sounds. |

**Anti-persona:** someone who wants live video, remote alerts or a smart-home integration; anyone expecting Kevin to replace an alarm.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** timers look fake; monitoring fees; a recent break-in nearby; dark evenings.
**Pull:** one device that looks convincingly alive from the street; privacy; Swiss build; one-time price.
**Habit:** "we leave a lamp on"; "the neighbour has a key".
**Anxiety:** will it work when I'm 1,000 km away; will it break after three months; will I manage the app.
