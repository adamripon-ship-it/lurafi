## Top themes (frequency × intensity)

### Theme 1 — "I want my home to always look occupied" (High confidence: S1, S3, S4, S6, S8, S10)
The job is not "an alarm". It is: from the street, at dusk, this home must read as lived-in. Customers judge the product by exactly that test.
- "Ever since our house was burgled, I've stopped deluding myself. […] That's why a deterrent's more important to me than any alarm system. I want my home to always look occupied; otherwise – especially during the darker months – it's practically an open invitation for thieves." — S1
- "When I first saw Kevin's work from the street in the dark, I was impressed. […] if I didn't know better, I'd actually believe that someone was walking through my flat." — S1
- "Really gives the impression of having someone in the room" / "Incredible light!" — S4 (verified buyer, translated from French)
- "Convincing deterrent effect" — S3 (verified buyer)
- "doen alsof je thuis bent is tegen inbraken het beste medicijn" (pretending you are home is the best medicine against burglary) — S8

### Theme 2 — The fear is not the theft, it is what comes after (High: S1, S9, S10)
- "Der Einbruch verursacht Stress bei den Opfern und das teilweise nachhaltig […] Deine Smart Home Kamera sagt Dir ja nur, hey es wird grade eingebrochen. Das ist zu spät." — S7 (founder, company voice)
- "drei Viertel der Betroffenen fühlen sich danach in der eigenen Wohnung unsicher. Jedes vierte Opfer leidet noch mindestens ein Jahr später unter Stress und Anspannung." — S10 citing KFN

## Ideal Customer Profile (per pillar, from the Golden Circle board S12)

**Why (owner):** change the way burglary is prevented; safeguard assets and mental health; protect your sanity by keeping people out of your safe spaces. **How:** add the missing link — proactive deterrence.

### Pillar 1 — Deterrence
ICP: households and small businesses in DE, NL, FR, CH, AT, BE, CZ, NO/IS that are regularly empty (commute, travel, second home, shop after hours), own or rent, have already tried timers or a smart bulb, and reject cameras or monitoring fees. Price tolerance ~€580 device (they compare with a Ring alarm kit — S1). Buys after a trigger: a burglary nearby or their own, the clocks going back, a booked holiday, a new second home.

### Pillar 2 — Safeguarding
ICP: the adult child or partner who buys for someone else — a parent living alone, a daughter in her first flat, a partner who is home alone at night. Motivated by worry, not by gadgets. Rejects anything that watches the loved one (cameras) or that the loved one cannot operate. Often the buyer sets it up; the resident only ever touches the three buttons.

## Personas

Each persona: who / trigger · WIIFM in their words · goals · what keeps them up at night · fears (plain) · suspicious of · the enemy · objections and counters · their exact words (cited).

### P1 — The burgled homeowner (Deterrence · preventing burglary) — confidence High
- **Who / trigger:** Owner-occupier, family, ground floor or house with balcony doors; was burgled or a neighbour was. Trigger: the break-in, or the first dark evenings of autumn.
- **WIIFM:** "I want my home to always look occupied" (S1). Not a siren after the fact — the burglar picks another house.
- **Goals:** stop being "an open invitation"; sleep on holiday; not turn the house into a bunker.
- **Keeps them up at night:** the dark months; balcony doors "can be broken into in no time as soon as the place looks empty" (S1).
- **Fears:** it happens again; the family stops feeling safe at home; "three quarters feel unsafe in their own flat afterwards" (S10).
- **Suspicious of:** gadgets that are "a moody gimmick" (S4); brands whose reviews are written by staff (S2); anything that needs Wi-Fi to work.
- **Enemy:** the opportunist who tests the doormat and the letterbox; dark windows.
- **Objections → counters:** "It can't raise the alarm" → true; Kevin is the prevention layer and complements the alarm you keep. "Only one room" → place it where the street looks in; add a second unit for a second facade. "Price of a Ring kit" → one-time purchase, no monitoring fee, 3-year warranty.
- **Their words:** "a deterrent's more important to me than any alarm system" (S1); "Unless Kevin McCallister's tormenting the thieves, ordinary windows and balcony doors can be broken into in no time as soon as the place looks empty." (S1)

### P2 — The commuter couple / holiday household (Deterrence · away from home) — confidence Medium-High
- **Who / trigger:** Two incomes, flat or terraced house empty 9–19 h and for holidays; trigger: a booked trip, "the vulnerable moment of arriving home alone at night" (S12), or clocks going back.
- **WIIFM:** leave and stop thinking about it; "so you can focus on your job, not your doorstep" (kevinswiss copy, company voice).
- **Goals:** one thing that runs itself; no six lamps on timers.
- **Keeps them up at night:** the flat "sieht leer aus" for two weeks; the overflowing letterbox (S10 sources).
- **Fears:** coming home to a broken door; holiday ruined.
- **Suspicious of:** subscriptions and "membership" upsells; apps that "loop in setup for hours" (S5); geofencing that "does not always work" (S3).
- **Enemy:** predictability — "the same lamp at the same time every night".
- **Objections → counters:** "Do I have to remember to switch it on?" → weekly schedule or geofencing; be honest that geofencing triggers at ~250–300 m (S1). "Will the neighbours hear it?" → sounds are normal-conversation level; light is the main effect (S1).
- **Their words:** "You can set a start/end time. It can be switched on remotely (smartphone)" (S4); "Practical to use once you've got the hang of getting it up and running." (S3)

### P3 — The second-home owner (Deterrence · holiday home / assets) — confidence Medium
- **Who / trigger:** owns a chalet, coast flat or boat that stands empty for weeks; trigger: end of season, a break-in in the village, an insurer's letter.
- **WIIFM:** every property looks lived-in between visits, from one app, without a caretaker.
- **Goals:** weekly occupancy rhythm; no monthly fees; check status from the city.
- **Keeps them up at night:** the property is a known-empty target for months; nobody to collect the post.
- **Fears:** damage found weeks later; insurer disputes.
- **Suspicious of:** devices that die after three months ("Broken after 3 months" — S4) with nobody to call.
- **Enemy:** the obviously empty house in the dark season.
- **Objections → counters:** "Reliability?" → 3-year warranty, 30-day returns, support address on every page. "Wi-Fi at the chalet is bad" → simulations play from the device; Wi-Fi is for setup and updates.
- **Their words:** "Cabins, holiday flats, and boats" (current site), "Set once per season" (current site) — company language; no verified owner quotes found → treat this persona as provisional.

### P4 — The small-business owner (Deterrence · business) — confidence Low (provisional)
- **Who / trigger:** shop, practice, small office or workshop empty at night; trigger: a break-in on the street, insurer requirement.
- **WIIFM:** the premises look staffed after closing; no monitoring contract.
- **Goals / fears / enemy:** as P3, with stock and equipment at stake.
- **Suspicious of:** consumer gadgets in a commercial setting.
- **Their words:** none found in public sources; kevinswiss lists "Offices, Shops and Warehouses" (company voice). Do not build copy claims on this persona beyond "works in homes, offices and shops".

### P5 — The adult child buying for a parent who lives alone (Safeguarding · grandparent) — confidence High
- **Who / trigger:** 45–60, lives elsewhere; parent 75+ alone in the family house; trigger: a fall, a scam call, a burglary in the parent's street, or the parent saying "I don't feel safe here at night".
- **WIIFM (in the buyer's words):** "make the grandparent seem less weak" (S12); the house "looks cared for, not vacant".
- **Goals:** something the parent never has to operate; nothing that watches the parent.
- **Keeps them up at night:** the parent alone in a dark house; being 400 km away.
- **Fears:** the parent is targeted precisely because they are old and alone; the parent refuses cameras and the child has nothing.
- **Suspicious of:** surveillance dressed as care; apps the parent cannot use — "eight out of ten older couples call their children or grandchildren" (S1).
- **Enemy:** the predator who picks the weak.
- **Objections → counters:** "Mum can't do apps" → three buttons on the device; the child sets schedules from their own phone. "She'll hate a camera" → there is none, and no microphone. "Is it dignified?" → it is a lamp and a speaker on the sideboard: "I can't help but picture it on the elegant sideboard of an older couple" (S1).
- **Their words:** S1 as above; "a completely new sense of security" (first Kevin.3 rating on Galaxus, 31 Oct 2024 — flagged by S2 as unverified/possibly manufacturer-side, so do not quote it in copy).

### P6 — The parent of a daughter in her first flat, or the woman living alone (Safeguarding · studying kid / living alone) — confidence Medium
- **Who / trigger:** student or young professional, alone in a new city; or her parents. Trigger: move-in day, a creepy incident, a friend's break-in.
- **WIIFM:** the flat "never feels, or sounds, alone" (current site); neighbours in the corridor see a lived-in door.
- **Goals:** feel safe at night without a boyfriend, a dog or a landlord's permission; no drilling.
- **Keeps them up at night:** "Nighttime anxiety is one of the most consistently reported challenges among solo dwellers, particularly women" (S11, secondary); posting a story from home "announces you're alone".
- **Fears:** someone knows she is alone; being followed home.
- **Suspicious of:** hype; anything loud through thin walls; anything that films her flat.
- **Enemy:** the stalker/predator who watches for the single light and the silence.
- **Objections → counters:** "Rental, can't install" → plug and play, no drilling. "Too expensive for a student" → the parent buys it; it moves with her.
- **Their words:** S11 compilation (secondary): timers on lamps in living areas "moving from room to room as they would in the evening". No verified first-person quotes about Kevin from this segment were found → provisional.

### P7 — The new parent / single parent at home in the evening (Safeguarding · "intrusion while occupants are home") — confidence Medium
- **Who / trigger:** upstairs with the baby or toddler at 20:00, ground floor dark; trigger: a knock at the door at night, a burglary while someone was home nearby.
- **WIIFM:** "downstairs still active"; another adult seems to be in the house (S12: "intrusion while occupants are home").
- **Fears:** being confronted while the children are in the house.
- **Suspicious of:** nursery cameras; anything streaming from inside the home.
- **Their words:** company language only (current site slides); no verified quotes → provisional.

## Messaging implications (feeds Step 2)

1. Lead with safety and control, name the fear in one line, pivot to presence and reassurance (S12 guideline; S10 shows fear is already high).
2. Say precisely what it does from the street at dusk — light, moving shadow, sound — and what it does not do (no alarm, no camera, no mic, one room).
3. Proof must be specific and attributed: the January 2026 Galaxus verdict, the 3-year warranty, the 30-day return, 70+ hours, 32 GB local storage, 9 W. Nothing else.
4. Setup honesty converts the sceptic: "plug in, place near the window, pair the app; if you'd rather not use an app, three buttons do the job."
5. French and Dutch pages must not imply French/Dutch voices.
