# mitipi.eu — Messaging rules (Phase 2, Step 2)

Derived from `.agents/product-marketing.md`, the customer research (02) and the Golden Circle brief. These rules are applied to every page and every language.

## 1. Safety first, fear named once
Lead with safety and reassurance. The fear may be named in one plain line, then the copy pivots to control, presence and reassurance (owner guideline; the KFN data shows fear is already high — amplifying it undercuts the promise). Banned as lead words: predators, stalkers, unthinkable.

## 2. Every H1 states the benefit and who it is for
Pattern: [who] + [what changes for them] + [how it is proven]. No generic "smart security" claims.

## 3. WIIFM before features
Feature → what it does → what it means for this persona (benefits ladder on every page). Features appear only as proof for a benefit already stated.

## 4. Only verifiable claims
Allowed sources: the live product facts (`assets/llms-full.txt`, `.cursor/skills/kevin-product-fact-check/product-facts.md`), the Galaxus review of 9 Jan 2026, the Shopify shipping/warranty settings, and figures supplied by the owner. Anything else goes to a "needs verification" list, not into copy. No invented statistics, testimonials or certifications.

## 5. Trust differentiators appear on every page
Swiss-engineered · no camera, no microphone · plays from the device, so it keeps working when Wi-Fi is jammed. Wording: "jammer-resilient", never "jammer-proof".

## 6. Honest limits
Kevin deters; it does not raise an alarm. One room per device. Placement matters (window visible from the street, wall or ceiling for shadows). Voices are in English, German and Swiss German; the app is in English, German, French and Dutch.

## 7. Customer language over company language
Use the buyers' own phrases: "looks occupied", "someone is home", "from the street", "the dark months", "no camera watching my mother". Avoid "AI-driven", "innovative", "ecosystem".

## 8. One primary action per page
CTA labels say what the reader gets: "Configure & buy Kevin", "See how it works", "Read the setup guide". Never "Learn more" or "Submit".

## 9. Specific numbers only where they change a decision
70+ hours, up to four weeks without repeating, 32 GB, 9 W, 3-year warranty, 30-day returns, free EU shipping. Nothing else.

## 10. Same meaning in every language
Translations keep facts and limits identical. Tone adapts (Sie in German, u in Dutch for safeguarding copy, vous in French, vy in Czech).
