## What is KEVIN® 3 and who is it for?

KEVIN® 3 ("Kevin") is a home-security device that prevents burglary instead of reacting to it. Placed by a street-facing window, it plays warm light, patented moving shadows and everyday household sounds so that, from outside, someone is clearly home. It is made for homes, flats, offices, shops and holiday properties that sit empty during the day, on holiday or between visits: people who live alone, families away on holiday, frequent travellers and expats, students in a first apartment, retirees, single parents, and owners of second homes.

## How Kevin works (three steps)

1. Place Kevin by a window with a wall or ceiling behind it for the shadows and connect the 3 m power cable.
2. In the free Kevin app, set a weekly schedule, or let geofencing start Kevin when your phone leaves the area.
3. While you are away, light, moving shadows and sound play from local storage, day and night, so the home looks lived-in from the street.
